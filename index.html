How this reading is built

Current planet positions come from the ephemeris. Planet-to-sign influence uses whole-sign relationships because no personal birth degree is collected. Planet-to-planet aspects use actual angular distance and configured orbs. Matching interpretation fragments are pulled from JSON. Aspect orb is the difference between the calculated angular separation and the exact aspect angle; aspects are shown only within their configured limits. Readings are interpretations of real astronomical configurations, not evidence that personal events will occur.

Today’s Rating

1–5 stars

An interpretive 1–5 indicator, not a prediction or measured probability. It combines weighted whole-sign relationships (up to four planets) with up to three degree-based aspects, applying the configured orbs. Ratings may repeat while the sky's relevant relationships stay similar.

How are today's ratings calculated?

Starting from 3 stars, the program combines a weighted average of the sign relationships (conjunction +0.2, sextile +0.65, trine +1, square −0.8, opposition −0.9, neutral 0) and up to three closest configured aspects involving planets assigned to each theme (trine +1, sextile +0.7, conjunction +0.15, square −0.8, opposition −0.9), weighted by proximity to exactness. The whole-sign weighted average is multiplied by 1.6; the sum of exactness-adjusted aspect values divided by the number of chosen aspects is multiplied by 0.8. Both contributions are added to a base of 3 before rounding and limiting to 1–5 stars. The exactness factor is 1 minus orb divided by that aspect’s configured allowed orb. These weights are editorial interpretation rules, not scientifically validated outcomes.
